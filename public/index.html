Modular Design

System Architecture

1. Ingestion & Validation

Automated extraction from UCI Capital Bikeshare archive. Strict schema verification and elimination of target leakage (casual and registered columns).

2. Feature Engineering

Extracts cyclical sine/cosine pairs for continuous hour/month representation, rush-hour flags, apparent temperature deltas, and wind-chill indices.

3. Inference & Warehouse

Real-time prediction engine enforcing physical value checks and non-negative counts. SQLite warehouse powered by SQLAlchemy for experiment and telemetry logging.
